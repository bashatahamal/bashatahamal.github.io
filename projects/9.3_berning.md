  outreach.

describe-content:
    - <strong>Leadflow, an AI Lead Generation Platform</strong>
    - Designed the platform's agentic AI core, where each capability (research, scoring, enrichment) is built as a reusable agent, callable as an API, a chat tool, or a workflow step.
    - Built an in-app AI agent with dozens of tools that searches, filters, sorts, and takes direct action on the lead review canvas (accepting or rejecting leads, merging duplicates, bulk field updates, pulling company research, and more).
    - Built a multi-step AI research agent that autonomously gathers and synthesizes company and market information to support lead scoring and outreach.
    - Built the core LLM-based lead scoring engine, then redesigned it into a multi-client architecture that scaled the platform to multiple clients with zero breaking changes.
    - Built a duplicate-detection and merge engine for companies and leads, with dry-run previews and transactional apply, wired into the agent as scoped merge tools.
    - Rebuilt the internal lead review tool from a single-file prototype into a modular React application with live job tracking, including its token-based design system.
    - Built the platform's background task management and notification system (in-app plus Telegram) covering every async workflow.
