describe-opening: >
  Berning builds AI-powered commercial systems for life science companies,
  the tools, reagents, and lab-software vendors that are strong at R&D but thin
  on sales. As a Software Engineer on the AI systems team I helped grow Leadflow
  from an early single-client scoring API into a full agentic platform: research
  agents, multi-client LLM scoring, deduplication, and a rebuilt lead review
  product, all feeding personalized, AI-generated outreach.

describe-content:
    - <strong>Leadflow, an AI Lead Generation Platform</strong>
    - Designed the platform's agentic AI core, where each capability (research, scoring, enrichment) is built as a reusable agent on an MCP tool layer, callable as an API, a chat tool, or a workflow step.
    - Built an in-app AI agent with dozens of tools that searches, filters, sorts, and takes direct action on the lead review canvas (accepting or rejecting leads, merging duplicates, bulk field updates, pulling company research, and more).
    - 'Gave the agent a real control surface: streaming replies that show its thinking and a live tool timeline, a composable skill system, and a per-conversation model and reasoning-effort picker shared across every chat surface.'
    - Wired structured questions into the agent, so when it lacks context it stops and asks instead of guessing.
    - Built a multi-step AI research agent that autonomously gathers and synthesizes company and market information to support lead scoring and outreach.
    - Built the core LLM-based lead scoring engine, then redesigned it into a multi-client architecture that scaled the platform to multiple clients with zero breaking changes.
    - Built the company intelligence behind scoring on a deterministic rubric, so a score could always be traced back to its reasons instead of being a black box.
    - Built a duplicate-detection and merge engine for companies and leads, with dry-run previews and transactional apply, wired into the agent as scoped merge tools.
    - 'Rebuilt the internal lead review tool from a single-file prototype into a modular React application: a spreadsheet-style canvas where reviewers filter, sort, bulk-edit, open company detail drawers, and leave comments, on a shared component and design-token foundation.'
    - Built the review canvas on a server-virtualized data table with live job tracking, so it stays responsive over large lead sets while background work runs.
    - Built the product's analytics views, a lead pipeline funnel and a daily output heatmap that stay in sync in real time, so the team could see what the agents were actually producing.
    - Built the platform's background task management and notification system, covering every async workflow.

describe-closing: '<strong>Tools used:</strong> Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, DeepAgents, MCP, PostgreSQL, Redis, React, TypeScript, Docker'
